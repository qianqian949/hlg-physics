{graph_summary}

YOUR TASK:

1. **Inferred Nodes** (new concepts NOT in the paper but highly relevant):

   - Foundational concepts or theories that underpin the research

2. **Inferred Relations**:
   - Connections between inferred nodes and existing nodes
   - Logical connections between existing nodes not found in paper
   - Transitive or compositional relationships from existing graph
   - Connections to inferred nodes that explain "why these concepts together"

IMPORTANT CONSTRAINTS:
- Only infer concepts that are HIGHLY relevant to understanding this specific graph
- Do NOT just add general ML/AI concepts - be specific to this research context
- Each inferred element must have clear relevance to the paper's focus
- Aim for 3-5 high-quality inferred nodes (not too many!)
- Prioritize concepts that reveal deeper insights, not obvious additions

Output in valid JSON with the schema:
