PAPERS AND THEIR CONCEPTS:

{papers_data}

YOUR TASK:

1. **SYSTEMATICALLY COMPARE ALL PAIRS OF PAPERS** - Don't just compare consecutive papers
   - For N papers, you should consider all N×(N-1)/2 possible paper pairs

3. Find ALL relations that are **grounded in paper content** - not just conceptual similarities. Do not limit the number of relations - find as many as are genuinely meaningful. 4. Focus on meaningful cross-paper connections that reveal:
   - How one paper extends, improves, or builds upon another
   - How papers address the same or related problems differently
   - How techniques from one paper could solve problems in another
   - How papers validate, support, or conflict with each other
   - How concepts from different papers complement each other

5. Base relations on ACTUAL PAPER CONTENT, not general knowledge

Output in valid JSON with the schema:

CROSS-PAPER RELATION TYPES:

Progress & Extension:
  "extends"           - Paper A extends or builds upon Paper B's work
  "improves"          - Paper A improves upon Paper B's approach
  "generalizes"       - Paper A generalizes Paper B's specific technique
  "specializes"       - Paper A provides specific case of Paper B's general approach

Problem & Solution:
  "addresses"         - Paper A addresses problem/limitation identified in Paper B
  "solves"            - Paper A solves problem discussed in Paper B
  "applies"           - Paper A applies technique/method from Paper B
  "combines"          - Paper A combines approaches from Paper B (and possibly others)

Validation & Evidence:
  "validates"         - Paper A validates claims/results from Paper B
  "supports"          - Paper A provides supporting evidence for Paper B
  "confirms"          - Paper A confirms findings from Paper B
  "contradicts"       - Paper A contradicts claims/findings from Paper B

Comparison & Alternatives:
  "compares-to"       - Papers explicitly compare approaches
  "alternative-to"    - Paper A presents alternative to Paper B's approach
  "conflicts"         - Papers present conflicting methodologies or conclusions

Conceptual Overlap:
  "related-to"        - Concepts are related across papers (use sparingly, be specific)
  "same-as"           - Concepts refer to the same thing (different terminology)

Note: Prefer specific relation types over generic ones like "related-to"

CONFIDENCE SCORING:

Rate each cross-paper relation confidence from 1-10 based on:
  * 9-10: Both papers explicitly discuss the connection
  * 7-8: Strong evidence of connection from both papers' content
  * 6-7: Reasonable inference from paper abstracts/conclusions
  * 3-4: Weak connection, mostly based on conceptual similarity
  * 1-2: Very speculative, minimal evidence in papers

IMPORTANT: Base confidence on EVIDENCE FROM BOTH PAPERS, not general research knowledge!
